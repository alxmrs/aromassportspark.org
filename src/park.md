## Sports Field

![](/assets/park-sportsfield-1.webp){.tile-photo}

In 2018, the ACCF and the Aromas Youth Soccer Club collaborated and focused to build a multipurpose field.
This sports field is important for the local tri-county residents to use for drop-in play activities, or
reserve for regular team sport practices, & games.

Around the beginning of February, the field will be temporarily closed so it can "rest" for 3 weeks, seeding
and filling in patches of the grass that need to be restored from the previous year's activities. Signs will
be displayed during this time. Field use will resume around the end of February.

![](/assets/park-sportsfield-2.webp){.tile-photo}

## Dog Park

::: eyebrow
Established July 2019 &middot; Open sunrise to sunset &middot; Join the Aromas Dog Park Group on Facebook
:::

![](/assets/park-dogpark-1.webp){.tile-photo}

It was formed under the leadership of local community members and built by volunteers. The park features two
separate areas for large and small dogs, a gated pen for entering and exiting, and available fresh water.

The Dog Park relies on volunteers for support, maintenance, and operation.

![Initial drawing of the dog park](/assets/park-dogpark-drawing.webp){.tile-photo}

The Dog Park was updated by Elijah T. for an Eagle Scout project from Troop 504. Elijah worked on
revitalizing the entrance to the Dog Park. The project consisted of painting, resurfacing, putting a roof on
the large bulletin board, planting shrubs, installing fresh mulch, painting the perimeter a dark blue,
installing solar lighting, and the placement of a plaque and boulder honoring the project.

Thank you, Elijah, for all the hard work you did to elevate the Dog Park to how it looks today.

![Many work days and volunteer hours led to its creation](/assets/park-dogpark-2.webp){.tile-photo}

## Pavilion

![](/assets/park-pavilion-1.webp){.tile-photo}

**Phase III of the Pavilion:** Landscaping is in the works! The ACCF has the plans and the scope of work
outlined for the beautification of the area surrounding the pavilion. In Spring 2026, ACCF seeded the ground
between the field and pavilion. ACCF is eager to complete the vision for the community park and is working on
the fundraising to complete the surrounding aspects of the pavilion.

**Phase II of the Pavilion:** The beautiful concrete floor was completed in Spring of 2024.

**Phase I of the Pavilion:** The main structure building project was completed in July 2023.

![](/assets/park-pavilion-2.webp){.tile-photo}
![](/assets/park-pavilion-3.webp){.tile-photo}
![](/assets/park-pavilion-4.webp){.tile-photo}

After two years of planning, engineering, and permitting, pavilion construction began on May 1st, 2023. Phase
I was completed at the end of July 2023. The Park would like to celebrate the efforts of those in our
community that have helped us along the way.

Board, would like to thank those that have contributed their time and expertise to make this project a
