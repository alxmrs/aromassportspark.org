## Donate

The park requires financial help from many sources. ACCF is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization and your
contribution is tax-deductible.

Please click on the 'Donate' button to make a contribution through a credit card or PayPal, or make a check
payable to:

## Purchase a Monument Donor Tile

![](/assets/get-involved-monument-tile.webp){.tile-photo}

Purchase a tile for the family, a loved one, a special person in the community, or leave a quote or
inspirational saying. Email the Park: [aromassportspark@gmail.com](mailto:aromassportspark@gmail.com)

Board meetings are held every third Thursday of the month at the Aromas Water District and are open to the
public. Meetings begin at 6:30 pm. Please keep public comment to under three minutes. No meetings in June and
December.

The Aromas Community Park is currently recruiting for more ACCF Board Members. Become involved and make a big
impact with the community for the park. If you are interested, contact ACCF at
[aromassportspark@gmail.com](mailto:aromassportspark@gmail.com)
